How it works

Three Steps to a Magical Night

I

Reserve Online

Choose your area, mark your home on the map, and pick a free half hour. It takes two minutes.

II

We Call to Confirm

A scout leader calls you before Christmas Eve to confirm your time and arrange your gift.

III

Santa Knocks

On the night, Santa arrives with his scouts, greets the children by name and hands over your gift.
